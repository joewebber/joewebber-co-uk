02 Professional Draft copy

Fifteen years of teams, systems and decisions.

I wanted to be a designer and learned to code as a workaround. The workaround became the career. Frontend, backend, cloud: I learn exactly enough of each to build the thing, then stop.

Prototyping, solving and architecting is the part I actually enjoy, which turns out to be management. I found that out by accident, which is how most people find it out. Fifteen years later: development teams built and scaled, onshore and offshore, and my name on what they ship.

Now it is product leadership: technology in service of business value, which in practice means fewer heroics and better defaults. Platforms, golden paths, and the deeply boring infrastructure that lets everyone else look fast.

Whether you write code or lead people, your career is something you get to shape.
